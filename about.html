ABOUT SPARK

A place to learn, create, and grow.

SPARK is a student-led IT team at Zarqa University. We bring students together to explore technology, practice teamwork, and turn ideas into projects.

🎯

Our Mission

Create a welcoming space where students learn by doing and build practical skills together.

🔭

Our Vision

Grow a creative student technology community known for collaboration and meaningful projects.

🤝

Our Values

Curiosity, respect, teamwork, responsibility, and continuous learning.
